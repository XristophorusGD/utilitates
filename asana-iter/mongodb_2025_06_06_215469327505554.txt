01:29 PM | Shivam from MongoDB: Thank you for your patience! When using an Identity Provider (IdP) to connect to your MongoDB cluster (like through Compass), MongoDB expects the authentication process to follow standard OIDC protocol. You correctly added the client ID during the Identity Provider setup in Atlas, but Atlas doesn’t require the client secret to be passed by the end user (like Compass).
​ 
 That said, if you’re seeing an error that says "client_id" and "client_secret" must authenticate, this usually means the MongoDB Identity Provider settings might not be fully or correctly configured thus request you to please refer to our documentation named: Authentication and Authorization with OIDC/OAuth 2.0 (https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/security-oidc/#authentication-and-authorization-with-oidc-oauth-2.0). Once done, Compass should redirect you and authenticate successfully using OIDC.
